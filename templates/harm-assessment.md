## 6. Publication test

Before publication answer:

1. Is each personal detail necessary to the public-interest finding?
2. Could the same finding be supported with less identifying information?
3. Does publication expose a person to foreseeable retaliation, stigma or operational risk?
4. Does the audience need raw material or only the verified finding?
5. Have sensitive annexes been separated from public output?
