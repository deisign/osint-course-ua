---

# Grade bands

- `90–100` — excellent / reproducible professional work
- `80–89` — strong pass
- `70–79` — pass with notable weaknesses
- `60–69` — revision required
- `<60` — fail

---

# Critical fail conditions

A submission cannot pass if it:

1. states that `@sivernyi_signal` is proven original creator;
2. ignores R1 as earlier than R2a;
3. labels inferred copying as observed forwarding;
4. claims that the bundle proves filming on 12 April 2026 near Belgorod;
5. ignores the edit from R2a to R2b;
6. provides a conclusion that cannot be traced to records in the bundle;
7. fabricates live Telegram evidence not present in the controlled dataset.

---

# Instructor note

The task is designed so that **restraint is part of the correct answer**. A student who produces a spectacular propagation graph with unsupported edges should score lower than a student who leaves several relationships unknown but documents the evidence correctly.
